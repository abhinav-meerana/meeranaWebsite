# AI visual language: what to show instead of clichés

## Replace these
| Cliché | Why it fails | Show instead |
|---|---|---|
| Robots, androids, humanoid heads | Says "AI" with no information; invites the swap-the-logo test | Model output (overlays, transcripts, drafts) |
| Glowing brains, neural-net wallpaper | Decoration with no truth content | Brand lattice line art (Meerana) or the camera's ground grid (YISBIR) |
| Globes and world networks | Generic; heavy (the live globe texture is 698 KB); implies a reach not evidenced | Deployment topology: devices → local node → stays inside the boundary |
| Purple-blue gradients, neon cyan blobs (metaballs) | Template signal; off-brand | Flat brand surfaces; one accent per surface |
| Blurred "dashboard" screenshots | Unreadable, unverifiable, often fake | A crisp DOM reconstruction with a provenance label |
| AI-generated product videos and covers | Invented UI and capabilities (DL-A03) | Recordings of the real product (DL-A01), or labelled illustrations |

| Kind | Examples | Status |

## Data-viz as decoration, with real numbers
- **Partner timeline:** 2022 Software AG · 2024 FPT Software · 2024 Commvault · 2025 CyberHive (placard years, EV ids).
- **Deployment matrix:** 5 products × {on-prem, own cloud, hybrid, offline/BLE}, ticks from evidence.
- **Index timeline:** 24 h sample with hit ticks, labelled sample.
- **Lattice:** the logo's isometric geometry drawn as an engineering diagram, labelled with the five operating environments.

**Rules:**
- Every encoded value is true, or the graphic is labelled sample.
- No axes without units. No growth curves without data.
- For real charts, follow the `dataviz` skill.
