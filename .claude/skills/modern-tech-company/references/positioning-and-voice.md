# Positioning and voice

## Positioning statement (internal)
- **For** government entities, enterprises and SMEs in the UAE and the region,
- **who** need to run gates, cameras, workplaces, processes and official correspondence with less paper and more control,
- **Meerana Technologies** is a Dubai-founded (2022) builder of AI-powered enterprise products, deployable on the customer's own infrastructure.
- **Unlike** global SaaS vendors, Meerana builds in the UAE, aligns with UAE programmes (e.g. Zero Government Bureaucracy for Tasree3), supports Arabic as a first-class language (Tasree3 site, Nazo app), and pairs products with consulting and engineering services.

**Evidence:** EV-020, EV-006/007, EV-461, EV-410, EV-415, Nazo demo (2026-10-04), EV-030–033.

## Boilerplates (proposed, for sign-off)

**25 words.** Meerana Technologies builds AI-powered enterprise products in the UAE — for access control, video intelligence, workplace operations, process management and official correspondence.

**50 words.** Founded in Dubai in 2022, Meerana Technologies builds AI-powered enterprise products — Wasel, Yisbir, Mandoobee, Tasree3 and Nazo — that can run on the customer's own infrastructure. Meerana also delivers consulting, bespoke development, technology partnerships and staff augmentation to put them to work.

## Voice attributes
| Attribute | Means | Sounds like | Not like |
|---|---|---|---|
| Confident | States what it does, plainly | "Yisbir indexes what is said in 50+ languages." | "Yisbir aims to revolutionise video." |
| Precise | Names objects, numbers and places | "Blue Bay Tower, Business Bay" · "on-prem, cloud or hybrid" | "cutting-edge global solutions" |
| Respectful | Formal-friendly; no slang; no exclamation marks | "Tell us what you want to run better." | "Let's crush it together!" |
| Bilingual-minded | Translatable sentences; no idioms | "From the front gate to the final approval." | "We've got you covered." |

## UAE market notes
- **Buyers:** government entities, semi-government bodies, regulated enterprises and SMEs. Data residency and sovereignty matter. Make deployment facts explicit per product (DL-P12).
- **Programmes:** reference a named UAE programme only where a product explicitly aligns, e.g. Tasree3 and **Zero Government Bureaucracy** (EV-410). Never imply government endorsement. **Never use government emblems.** The Nazo demo's tenant emblem must not appear in marketing.
- **Arabic:**
  - The brand specifies Almarai (Meerana) and an Arabic face per product.
  - Arabic launches in phase 2 (DL-L01). Build RTL-ready now.
  - Use the product-name meanings where confirmed: Yisbir (probing), Tasree3 (acceleration) (DL-P11).
- **Dates and numbers:** English pages use "4 October 2026" and Western digits. Arabic pages follow DL-L02.
