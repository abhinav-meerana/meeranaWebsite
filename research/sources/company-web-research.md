**Confidence legend**
- **High**: a primary, official or independent editorial source that I fetched and read directly, or two independent sources that agree.
- **Medium**: one of these:
  - a company self-claim (website or LinkedIn) that matches independent context;
  - an official page I know only from a search-index snippet, because the page was blocked from this network.
- **Low**: a single self-claim, sponsored content, an aggregator, or an inference.
- **Unverified**: no supporting public evidence found.

## Key findings at a glance

1. **Identity**
   - **Verified:** Meerana was founded in 2022 in Dubai. The founder and CEO is Matar Almehairi. The UAE operating name is "Meerana Information Technology" (Arabic: ميرانا لتقنية المعلومات).
   - **Not public:** the licensing authority (DED/DET), the exact legal form ("LLC" wording) and any parent "group" could not be verified.
   - **Office number conflict:** the website and draft say #1704, while LinkedIn says unit 1706.
2. **Locations**
   - **UAE:** HQ verified.
   - **India:** an active LLP was verified.
   - **UK:** a UK company at the address shown on meerana.ae was **dissolved on 25 Nov 2025**.
   - **Egypt:** appears only in company boilerplate.
   - **KSA:** no evidence found.
   - **"5+ locations / 60+ staff / 40+ alliances / 50+ clients":** none can be verified. The site's own logo wall has 32 logos in total.
3. **Awards (resolved)**
   - **2023 Future 100:** verified. Meerana was selected for the inaugural list (UAE Ministry of Economy and Government Development and the Future Office).
   - **2023 MOHRE award:** verified. It is the Emirates Labour Market Award (1st edition), sub-category "Workforce Wellbeing and Quality of Life". It was **not** a "Best employee environment" award.
   - **Sept 2024 Doha honour:** verified. Meerana was honoured as the UAE's best small enterprise at the GCC Labour Ministers Committee ceremony in Doha. The draft's "Best SME in GCC, Qatar 2024" and "Best SME in UAE, MOHRE 2024" are **one honour**, and "Best in GCC" overstates it.
4. **ISO 27001 / 22301:** no public evidence that Meerana holds either certificate. The only "ISO 27001" in Meerana-related press belongs to CyberHive, not Meerana.
5. **Partners verified:**
   - Software AG (PartnerConnect, 2022)
   - FPT Software (MoU, Aug 2024)
   - Commvault (Value Added Partner, Oct 2024)
   - CyberHive (MoU, Jan 2025)
   - Botminds ("Regional" partner listing)
   - Dubai Chambers: supporter and programme host, not a vendor.
   - **Not verified:** SAP (no Partner Finder listing, so the "SAP Gold Partner" badge is unsupported), Microsoft (no directory listing), Dell, Cisco, Huawei, Kofax, Confluent, AgilePoint, SAS, Avaya, ZingHR, Moro Hub, Digital DEWA, DigitalX and all named clients.
6. **Events**
   - **High:** Vietnam trade mission (May 2024), GITEX Global 2022 and 2024 (partnership announcements), Dubai Business Forum USA (Nov 2025).
   - **Medium (self-reported):** COMEUP Seoul (Nov 2023), VivaTech (May 2024), Jakarta (May 2024), Lithuania (Aug 2024).
   - **Low / unverified:** SPIEF panel, China visit, Nord Security visit, and a kiosk at a Dubai Chambers pavilion during GITEX or Expand North Star 2024.

- the certificate (PDF);
- the certificate number;
- the certification body and its accreditation body (for example EIAC, UKAS or IAS);
