01 How the index is built

From raw footage to a searchable record.

  1. 01 · Upload

    Upload content

    Footage from your cameras and archives, on your infrastructure.

  2. 02 · Process

    AI processing

    Speech is transcribed by speaker, text in frame is read, and objects are indexed.

  3. 03 · Search

    Search and discover

    Find the moment by phrase, on-screen text, object or metadata.

  4. 04 · Export

    Export and share

    Secure downloads, behind access control.

02 Search the way you think

Seven ways to find one moment.

SearchFindsExample
PhraseExact words in transcripts, in more than 50 languages.
On-screen textWords on signs, boards and documents in frame.
ObjectsThings the model detects in frame.
MetadataCamera, location, date and time.CAM-04 · 06:00–08:00
SimilarityMoments that resemble one you pick.more like 09:31:52
Fuzzy and synonymClose spellings and related words still match.“permit aproved”
VectorMeaning rather than exact wording.“crew briefing on hot work”

Prototype note · Phrase, metadata, similarity, fuzzy and synonym, and vector are EV-442's search types; face search is left out (DL-P07). On-screen text comes from EV-442's document scanning with OCR, and objects from EV-136 object detection. The descriptions paraphrase those names, so confirm the wording with the product team. Only the first three run in this sample.
