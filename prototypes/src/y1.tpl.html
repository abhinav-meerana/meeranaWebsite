CH 01 00:00 · The problem

Hours of footage. One minute that matters.

YISBIR flags the moments your team needs to see while they happen, and indexes the rest so any minute can be found later.

CH 02 00:42 · What it watches for

Six site-safety detections, on the cameras you have.

Missing helmet
Workers without the required head protection.
Missing vest
PPE violations in active work zones.
Unsafe work practices
Risky behaviour and unsafe activity on site.
Restricted-zone entry
An alert when someone enters a hazardous area.
Falls and incidents
Falls, accidents and emergencies.
Workers in hazardous areas
People working near hazards and danger zones.

Prototype note · The six classes are EV-134 verbatim. The live site's security list (after-hours intrusion, perimeter breaches, vehicle movement anomalies) is in research/sources/live-site-extract.md but not yet in the evidence register. Faces, gender and plates are not shown (DL-P07).
